The Big 5 Personality Traits

It is important to note that each of the five primary personality traits represents a range between two extremes. For example, extraversion represents a continuum between extreme extraversion and extreme introversion. In the real world, most people lie somewhere in between.

While there is a significant body of literature supporting these primary personality traits, researchers don't always agree on the exact labels for each dimension. That said, these five traits are usually described as follows.

Openness
(openness to experience) imagination, insight, curiosity.
  • Curious about the world
  • Eager to learn
  • Drawn to new experiences
  • Adventurous · Creative

Low in trait: more traditional, struggle with abstract thinking.

HIGH
  • Very creative
  • Open to trying new things
  • Focused on new challenges
  • Happy with abstract concepts
LOW
  • Dislikes new concepts
  • Resists new ideas
  • Not very imaginative
  • Dislikes theory / abstract
Conscientiousness
self‑discipline, organization, goal‑directed.
  • Organized
  • Mindful of details
  • Thoughtful
  • Conscious of deadlines
Low scorers: less structured, procrastinate, miss deadlines.
HIGH
  • Spends time preparing
  • Finishes tasks right away
  • Pays attention to detail
  • Enjoys set schedule
LOW
  • Dislikes structure & schedules
  • Messes, doesn't take care
  • Fails to return things
  • Procrastinates
  • Fails to complete tasks
Extraversion
social, talkative, assertive, engagement with the world.
  • Outgoing
  • Energized by social
  • Friendly
Low (introversion): reserved, drained by social, need quiet to recharge.
HIGH
  • Enjoys being center of attention
  • Likes to start conversations
  • Enjoys meeting new people
  • Wide social circle
  • Finds it easy to make friends
  • Energized around others
  • Says things before thinking
LOW (introvert)
  • Prefers solitude
  • Exhausted by lots of social
  • Difficult to start conversation
  • Dislikes small talk
  • Thinks before speaking
  • Dislikes being center
Agreeableness
compassion, cooperativeness, kindness, trust.
  • Cooperative
  • Empathetic
  • Helpful

Low: more competitive, sometimes manipulative.

HIGH
  • Great interest in others
  • Cares about others
  • Empathy & concern
  • Enjoys helping others' happiness
  • Assists those in need
LOW
  • Little interest in others
  • Doesn't care how others feel
  • Not interested in others' problems
  • Insults & belittles
  • Manipulates to get what they want
Neuroticism
negative emotions: anxiety, stress, worry, instability.
  • Mood swings
  • Anxiety
  • Irritability
  • Sadness

Low scorers: more stable, emotionally resilient.

HIGH
  • Experiences a lot of stress
  • Worries about many things
  • Gets upset easily
  • Dramatic mood shifts
  • Feels anxious
  • Struggles to bounce back
LOW
  • Emotionally stable
  • Deals well with stress
  • Rarely sad or depressed
  • Doesn't worry much
  • Very relaxed
