Quezon Province

A Beautiful and Memorable Place in the Philippines

Quezon Province is located in the CALABARZON region of Southern Luzon, Philippines. It is bordered by the provinces of Aurora to the north, Laguna and Batangas to the west, and Camarines Norte and Camarines Sur to the east across the Pacific Ocean. The province is known for its long coastline, beautiful beaches, and mountainous landscapes, making it one of the most scenic provinces in the country.

Quezon Province was originally called “Tayabas” during the Spanish colonial period. It was officially established as a province in 1901 under American rule. In 1946, it was renamed Quezon Province in honor of Manuel L. Quezon, the second President of the Philippines and the leader of the Commonwealth government. Today, the province is governed by local government units under the Republic of the Philippines and continues to preserve its cultural heritage, historic churches, and traditional festivals.

Quezon Province is a memorable place for me because of its peaceful environment and natural beauty. It offers a relaxing escape from busy city life with its beaches, waterfalls, and countryside views. I also admire the warmth and hospitality of the people living there. Every visit feels refreshing and meaningful, making it a place I would always want to return to.
